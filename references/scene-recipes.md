# Scene recipes

Use these originals to identify the ordinary photographic exterior, the transparent boundary, and the small working space. Apply the relevant depth cues to the brief.

## Vase as a café

[Original reference image](images/vase-cafe.png)

The tabletop, cast shadow, and daisies above the open neck establish a flower vase at ordinary scale. The stems continue behind the thick lip into the room and give the eye a continuous path from outside to inside. A figure sits on the lip with legs descending into the opening. Below, the barista works at a compact counter and a guest sits with a cup. The curved sides and thick base remain visible around the café. Daylight breaks across the counter, interior floor, and tabletop, while a small pendant helps locate the interior.

**Adaptation.** Keep an open stretch of glass between the rim and the main interior activity where the form allows. Choose a stem, limb, or familiar object whose path makes the threshold obvious. Treat the rim, the outside shadow, and the inside floor as separate points in depth. Use a small local lamp only if it helps identify the function.

## Sphere as a metro station

[Original reference image](images/sphere-metro.png)

A clear glass sphere encloses a platform, rails, bench, station sign, waiting passengers and a train at the platform. The platform slab and parallel rails establish depth. The train door and waiting poses make the transit function immediately readable. Figures stand or sit on the platform, behind the curved glass boundary. Bright rim refractions, window-shaped highlights and warm caustics on the tabletop connect the miniature to the photographic sphere.

**Adaptation.** Retain a recognizable vehicle, boarding edge and grounded passengers. Let the near spherical wall slightly shift one rail line or the edge of a coat near the curve, with the boarding action visible through the clearer center.

## Perfume bottle as a lift

[Original reference image](images/perfume-bottle-lift.png)

A rectangular perfume bottle contains a cylindrical lift with a small adult standing on its raised floor. Another adult waits outside. Vertical rails, a lit ceiling and the suspended floor make the lift legible. The figure's feet meet the floor, while transparent cabin edges, bottle walls and reflections overlap the scene. Amber liquid at the base and golden light through thick glass produce matching tabletop caustics.

**Adaptation.** Keep the suspended floor and vertical guides readable. Position a cuff or bag edge behind a single bottle bevel so the bevel shifts a small section of its outline. Let light through the glass fall across the cab floor.

## Drinking glass as a pool

[Original reference image](images/tumbler-pool.png)

One adult sits on the rim of the tumbler with legs in the water, and another swims below the surface. The elliptical rim, waterline, swimmer's horizontal pose, bubbles and surface ripples identify the activity. The waterline crosses the seated figure's legs, while glass walls, underwater tint and refracted highlights layer over the swimmer. Sunlight creates bright caustics and a long tabletop shadow.

**Adaptation.** Anchor the action to the rim or water. Use the air-to-water transition to shift a submerged leg contour and carry a soft ripple caustic across the pool floor or swimmer.

## Handwritten title treatment

Use the upper-left corner of the [metro original](images/sphere-metro.png) for lettering. The first line is a short dark gesture. The second line opens out across the page with a loose left alignment and uneven character spacing. A small ochre train sits beside the short line, a single olive stroke sweeps below the Chinese, and a quiet English aside sits lower. Compare the compact group and its contrast against the pale backdrop at thumbnail size.

Adapt the structure to the exact supplied words. For the reading-room copy `借一本有香气的书`, break after `借一本`, draw a small ochre open book beside it, and let `有香气的书` occupy the longer line. Keep the illustration and English below the Chinese in visual weight. In the exterior, a canvas tote with a visible book on the tabletop connects the real foreground to the reading activity inside. Choose a single prop with an identifiable outline and a contact shadow.
